**App Review notes (Notes alanının başına)**
VERSION 1.1: adds auto-renewable subscriptions (weekly and monthly, 1-week free trial) and new features.
- The first schedule upload and the first script upload are free; later uploads need the subscription. Paywall: Settings (top-left icon) › "Planları gör", or upload a second schedule PDF. Terms of Use (EULA), Privacy Policy and Restore Purchases are on the paywall. Restore and Manage Subscription are also in Settings (top-left icon).
- Without a subscription the built-in sample schedule ("Örnek programla dene") and everything already uploaded stay fully usable.
- Users who bought the paid version 1.0 keep full access without subscribing (checked with AppTransaction: original build and purchase date).
- Notifications are optional: Settings › "Akşam hatırlatması" schedules a local reminder the evening before a shooting day. No push server is used.
- The app still collects no data.
