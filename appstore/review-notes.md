2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Set Pro ("Set Programım") is for actors working on Turkish TV series and films. Each week the production sends a long set schedule (a PDF "call sheet" listing every scene to be shot that week for the whole cast). Actors have to search these pages by hand for their own scenes. The app reads the PDF on the device, takes the actor's character name and lists only that character's scenes, day by day and in shooting order, with the synopsis, other characters in the scene, costume notes and page counts. On set the actor can mark scenes as shot and highlight the scene being shot now.
It is not made for a specific company or its employees: any actor can use it with schedules in the common Turkish production format (columns such as SAHNE, ZAMAN, MEKAN, OYUNCULAR, ÖZET).

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login is needed and no sample file needs to be downloaded.
- Launch the app and tap "Örnek programla dene" (Try with a sample program). A fictional weekly schedule for the character "Kaptan" is loaded into the current week.
- The top card shows the weekly summary; tap it to expand per-day counts.
- Tap a day in the top strip to jump to that day.
- Tap a scene to open its details (synopsis, characters, notes).
- Swipe a scene left to mark it as shot; swipe right to unmark it.
- Double-tap a scene to mark it as "now shooting" (ŞİMDİ); the next scene is shown as SIRADAKİ.
- Tap "Tüm program" at the top to see every scene of the schedule, with the actor's own scenes highlighted.
- To load a real schedule: tap the yellow + button, type a character name, choose a PDF from Files and tap "Programı Oku" (Read schedule).
- Uploaded schedules can be deleted from "Yüklenen programlar" (tap the date title at the top to open the week panel).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None for core functionality. There is no backend, analytics, advertising, authentication, payment processor or AI service. PDFs are parsed on the device with the bundled open-source pdf.js library (Apache-2.0). Only if the bundled reader fails to open a file, the app downloads the same open-source library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com; no user data or files are sent. All data stays on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app is available only in Türkiye and its interface is in Turkish. It works the same everywhere; there are no regional differences in features or content.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no third-party protected material. The sample schedule shipped in the app is entirely fictional and was created by the developer. Users only open their own schedule files, which stay on their device.
